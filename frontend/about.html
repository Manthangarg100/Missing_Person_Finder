About FindTrace

FindTrace is a revolutionary platform that leverages artificial intelligence and community collaboration to help locate missing persons. By combining advanced face recognition technology with human verification and trusted authority integration, we ensure accurate and responsible outcomes in the search for the missing.

Our Mission

Our mission is to reunite families and bring closure to communities by providing a reliable, AI-powered tool for missing person searches. We believe that technology, when used responsibly, can make a significant difference in solving one of society's most pressing issues.

How It Works

FindTrace utilizes state-of-the-art facial recognition algorithms to analyze uploaded images against a vast database of missing persons reports. Community members can submit sightings, which are then verified by trusted users and relatives before being escalated to authorities.

The process involves three key steps: AI Analysis for initial matches, Human Verification to confirm sightings, and Official Action where only verified leads reach police and NGOs.

Privacy & Security

We prioritize user privacy and data security, ensuring that all information is handled with the utmost care and in compliance with legal standards. Your data is protected, and we only share verified information with authorized parties.
