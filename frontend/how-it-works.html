How FindTrace Works

A verified, multi-step approach that combines artificial intelligence, community validation, and official authorities to ensure accuracy.

1. AI Face Matching

Uploaded photos are analyzed using face recognition algorithms. The system generates a match percentage across reported cases, CCTV frames, and public images.

→

2. Community Verification

Potential matches are shared with verified relatives and trusted community members. They confirm or reject sightings using a simple Yes / No response.

→

3. Police & NGO Action

Only verified and high-confidence cases are forwarded to authorized police stations and NGOs, enabling faster and more reliable intervention.
